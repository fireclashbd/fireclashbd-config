## 4. Fair Play Policy

Fair gameplay is strictly enforced.

The following activities are strictly prohibited:

* Hacking
* Cheats
* Scripts
* Mod APK usage
* Emulator abuse
* Exploiting bugs
* Fake results or edited screenshots
* Unfair teaming
* Any suspicious gameplay activity

If cheating or suspicious activity is detected:

* Your account may be permanently banned
* Your tournament access may be removed
* Your wallet balance may be frozen
* You may lose withdrawal access permanently

A banned account cannot:

* Log in

* Withdraw money
* Recover rewards

All admin decisions regarding cheating investigations are final.
